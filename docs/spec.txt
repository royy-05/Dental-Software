Kivi Health Booking Platform: Scope of Work

Nov 5, 2026 · @Jaydeb Barman

A multi-clinic appointment booking SaaS for healthcare practices, with three layers: a patient booking widget, a clinic panel for each practice, and a super admin panel for the platform owner. Phase 1 is a clickable prototype; production follows once the prototype is approved.

1. Platform at a glance

One platform, three layers, with each clinic's data kept separate.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Layer                    Used by                               Purpose
  ------------------------ ------------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------------------------
  Super admin panel        Platform owner team                   Onboard clinics, set plans and features, monitor every clinic

  Clinic panel             Clinic owners, managers, front desk   Configure booking, manage appointments, review performance

  Patient booking widget   Patients                              Book an appointment from the clinic's link or website
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-   Each clinic gets its own booking link (example: book.domain.com/clinic-name), website embed code, branding and settings.

-   One login can access several clinics and switch between them from a clinic selector in the top bar.

-   Every booking carries its source (Google Ads, Google Business Profile, Reserve with Google, Website, or a custom referrer) so results can be tracked end to end.

2. Roles and permissions

Six roles, each limited to what its job needs.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Role             Layer               Access                                                                                    Restrictions
  ---------------- ------------------- ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ----------------------------------------------------
  Super Admin      Super admin panel   All clinics, plans, features, users, global settings, platform analytics, impersonation   None

  Support Agent    Super admin panel   View clinics, tickets, logs; impersonate with reason logged                               No billing, no plan or feature changes, no deletes

  Clinic Owner     Clinic panel        Everything in their clinic(s), staff users, billing view, all reports                     Cannot see other clinics

  Clinic Manager   Clinic panel        Reasons, providers, insurances, templates, settings, appointments, reports                No billing, cannot remove the owner

  Front Desk       Clinic panel        Appointments (view, confirm, reschedule, cancel), patient details                         No settings, templates or reports

  Patient          Booking widget      Book, reschedule or cancel own appointment from email/SMS links                           No login required
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

-   Super Admin can create custom roles later by toggling permissions per module.

-   A Hide PHI switch masks patient names, phone numbers and emails on screen for any clinic user (useful for screen sharing).

3. Patient booking widget

Patients book in four steps, with an optional provider step. What each step shows is set per clinic (section 4).

Booking steps

1.  Reason for visit

    -   Reasons grouped under New Patient and Existing Patient (sections or tabs, per theme)

    -   Card per reason: name, short description, duration, "View details" for longer text

    -   Only active reasons with at least one bookable provider are shown

2.  Provider (optional, off by default)

    -   Pick a provider or "First available"

3.  Date and time

    -   Month calendar with available, selected and unavailable days

    -   Slots grouped by Morning, Afternoon, Evening, with the clinic time zone shown

    -   Slots respect provider hours, operatories, blockouts, minimum notice, booking window and buffers

4.  Patient details

    -   "I'm booking for someone else" option

    -   First name, last name, date of birth, mobile, email, message

    -   Insurance Yes/No; if Yes: carrier, subscriber full name, subscriber ID, subscriber date of birth

    -   Out-of-network, not-accepted and other-insurance warning messages

    -   Custom intake questions and optional file upload

    -   SMS consent checkbox with Terms and Privacy Policy links

    -   Optional OTP check of phone or email

5.  Review and confirm

    -   Summary of reason, provider, date, time and patient, with edit links

    -   Slot held for 5 minutes during checkout to prevent double booking

After booking

-   Default confirmation page (add to calendar, address, directions, call button) or redirect to a custom thank-you URL, per clinic or per referrer, with a 0 to 20 second delay

-   Confirmation email and SMS to the patient; alert to the clinic

-   Reschedule and cancel links in every message, within the clinic's cancellation window

Delivery and design

-   Hosted page per clinic, with referrer tracking in the URL (example: ?referrer_id=2)

-   Website embed: inline, popup, or floating "Book Online" button

-   Three themes: Compact, Full Page, Reason First

-   Branding: logo, header color, primary and secondary button colors

-   Mobile-first, accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA), single language for now

-   Footer with clinic name, phone, address and Terms link

-   Step-by-step drop-off tracking that feeds Form Analytics

4. Step and form builder

Each clinic controls every step of its booking flow without code, since every practice runs differently.

Steps

-   Turn steps on or off: Provider, Insurance, Intake questions, Review

-   Reorder steps: reason first, provider first, or date first

-   Edit each step's title, subtitle and button text

-   Choose how reasons display: grouped sections, tabs, or one list

Fields

-   Add custom fields: short text, long text, dropdown, radio, checkbox, date, phone, email, number, file upload, consent

-   Per field: label, placeholder, help text, required or optional, default value, show or hide

-   Drag to reorder; half-width or full-width layout

-   Built-in fields (name, date of birth, phone, email) can be renamed or made optional, not deleted

Conditional rules

-   Show a field or step only when:

    -   Patient type is New or Existing

    -   A specific reason is selected

    -   Insurance answer is Yes or No

    -   A previous answer equals a set value

    -   The booking is for someone else

-   Per-reason questions (example: "Which tooth is hurting?" only for Emergency Exam)

Messages and booking rules

-   Information for patient, paid appointment note, no-insurance message

-   Consent and legal text, custom Terms and Privacy Policy URLs

-   Minimum notice (hours), booking window (days ahead), slot interval, buffer between visits, daily booking cap, cancellation window

-   Accepting patients on/off, with a message shown while paused

Preview and versions

-   Live preview on mobile and desktop while editing

-   Save draft, publish, roll back to an earlier version

-   Starter templates by specialty (dental, physio, dermatology), published by Super Admin, that clinics copy and edit

5. Clinic panel

Eleven modules for running bookings day to day, all scoped to the clinic selected in the top bar.

Top bar (every page)

-   Clinic selector, Hide PHI switch, notifications bell, user menu

-   Raise a Ticket (goes to Super Admin support queue)

5.1 Home

-   Date range: Today, This week, This month, custom

-   Appointment trend chart with total and % change vs previous period

-   Patient type split: New vs Returning

-   Latest 5 appointments with View All

5.2 Appointments

-   Tabs: Appointments and Declined

-   Columns: date scheduled, patient type, provider, patient, reason, source, appointment date and time, status

-   Search by name, phone or email; filters for date, status, provider, reason, source, patient type

-   Sort, show/hide columns, 10/25/50 per page, CSV export

-   Statuses: Unconfirmed, Confirmed, Complete, Cancelled, No-show, Declined

-   Calendar view by day and week, per provider

-   Detail drawer: patient info, insurance, intake answers, files, source and UTM tags, message log, status history, internal notes

-   Actions: confirm, reschedule, cancel, decline with reason, mark complete or no-show, resend confirmation

-   Manual booking by staff for phone-in patients

5.3 Reasons for visit

-   List: drag to reorder, name, New/Returning badges, providers, operatories, duration, active switch, edit, duplicate, delete

-   Editor: name, description, duration, patient type, providers, operatories, color, estimated value, online or staff-only, per-reason questions, per-reason redirect URL, notice override

5.4 Providers

-   List: photo, display name, ID, role, operatories, edit; selector for which providers show online

-   Editor: display name, photo, role (Doctor, Hygienist, Assistant), bio, weekly hours, breaks, time off, operatories, reasons offered

5.5 Schedule and operatories

-   Clinic hours, holidays, blockouts

-   Operatories (rooms or chairs): name, active, linked providers and reasons; stops a room being double booked

5.6 Insurances

-   Pick carriers from the master list; add custom carriers (comma-separated bulk add); remove

-   Per-carrier status: in network, out of network, not accepted

-   Settings: accept other insurance, optional insurance fields, extra questions, and warning messages for other, out-of-network and not-accepted plans

5.7 Templates

-   Email and SMS version for each event: booked (new patient), booked (returning), booking request, confirmation request, declined, cancelled, rescheduled, reminder 24 hours, reminder 2 hours, practice notification, patient form request, form acknowledgment, PMS not connected alert

-   Editor: subject, rich text, placeholders ({{patient_name}}, {{practice_name}}, {{rfv_name}}, {{apt_date}}, {{start_time}}, {{provider_name}}, {{reschedule_link}}), SMS character count, send test, reset to default, on/off per event

5.8 Form Analytics

-   Date range and source filter

-   Totals: form visits, appointments booked, drop-offs, conversion rate

-   Drop-off by step and by reason, with counts and %

-   Visit and booking heatmaps by weekday and hour, with peak times

5.9 Performance

-   Overview: bookings, completed, cancelled, no-shows, show rate, change vs previous period

-   By source: visits, bookings, conversion rate for Google Ads, Google Business Profile, Reserve with Google, Website, custom referrers

-   Ad ROI: monthly spend per source entered by the clinic or agency; cost per booking and per completed visit

-   Patients: new vs returning trend, new patient growth

-   Reasons: bookings, completion rate, estimated value

-   Providers: bookings, utilization (booked vs available hours), completion rate

-   Timing: average lead time from booking to visit; fill rate for next 7 and 14 days

-   Export as CSV or PDF; scheduled monthly report by email

5.10 Settings

-   Appearance: logo, practice name, phone, email, address, description, theme, colors, live preview

-   Widgets: default link, custom slug, embed code, after-booking redirect URL and delay

-   Confirmation: which statuses count as confirmed, send confirm prompt or not, statuses written back to the PMS

-   Legal and compliance: Privacy Policy and Terms (edit, view, copy, on/off) or custom URLs

-   Booking flow and scheduling: opens the step and form builder (section 4), auto sync, block scheduling, blockouts to skip, unarchive patient on booking

-   Notifications: SMS alerts to clinic, notify patient after booking, reminders on/off, extra alert emails, alternate SMS number

-   Referrer: list (name, ID, source type, redirect URL), add, edit, copy tracked link, delete

-   Reserve with Google: on/off, website URL

-   Payment: Stripe connect (later phase)

-   Users: invite staff, assign role, deactivate

5.11 Support

-   Raise a Ticket with category, priority, screenshot; ticket history and replies

6. Super admin panel

The platform owner's control room: onboard and manage every clinic, set what each can use, and watch the whole platform.

6.1 Dashboard

-   Clinics: active, trial, paused, churned

-   Platform bookings, conversion rate and show rate this period vs last

-   Revenue: MRR, new and churned MRR, overdue invoices

-   SMS and email usage vs credits

-   Health alerts: failed messages, sync errors, clinics with zero bookings in 7 days

6.2 Clinics

-   List: name, plan, status, owner, created date, bookings this month, last login

-   Add clinic wizard: details, booking slug, time zone, owner invite, plan, starter template by specialty, import reasons and providers (CSV)

-   Edit, pause, suspend, soft delete with 30-day restore

-   Clone settings from another clinic

-   Log in as clinic (impersonation), reason required and logged

6.3 Clinic groups

-   Group several locations under one owner (multi-location practices)

-   Group-level users and combined reports

6.4 Plans and billing

-   Plans with limits: locations, providers, SMS credits, modules

-   Trials, coupons, invoices, payment status, dunning emails

-   Billing through Stripe (later phase); manual invoicing until then

6.5 Feature flags

-   Turn modules on or off per plan or per clinic: Performance, Reserve with Google, payments, OTP, file uploads, custom domain

6.6 Users

-   All users across clinics: role, clinics, last login, 2FA status

-   Reset password, force 2FA, deactivate

-   Super admin team users: Super Admin and Support Agent roles

6.7 Global libraries

-   Master insurance carrier list that clinics pick from

-   Default email and SMS templates

-   Starter booking flows and default reasons by specialty

-   Legal page templates (Privacy Policy, Terms)

6.8 Messaging

-   SMS and email provider settings, sender names and numbers

-   SMS registration status per clinic (US 10DLC), credit limits, delivery logs, bounce and opt-out lists

6.9 Integrations

-   Reserve with Google feed status per clinic

-   PMS connectors and sync health (later phase)

-   Analytics and ad tracking defaults (GA4, Google Ads, Meta Pixel)

6.10 Support desk

-   Tickets from clinics: assign, reply, status, priority, response time

-   Announcement banner to all or selected clinics

6.11 Platform analytics

-   Compare clinics side by side: bookings, conversion, show rate, source mix, ad cost per booking

-   Growth: new clinics per month, bookings per clinic, retention

-   Export CSV

6.12 Audit log and system settings

-   Audit log: who did what, when, from which IP; patient data access log

-   Platform branding and white-label: logo, colors, "Powered by" text, email sender domain

-   Custom domains per clinic, data retention period, backups, maintenance mode

7. Notifications, tracking and integrations

Every booking triggers messages to the patient and clinic, and carries its marketing source through to reports. In the prototype these are simulated.

Notifications

-   Channels: email and SMS, each event on or off per clinic

-   To patient: booking received, confirmed, confirmation request, reminders (24 hours and 2 hours, times adjustable), rescheduled, cancelled, declined, form request

-   To clinic: new booking alert (email and SMS), cancellation alert, daily summary of tomorrow's schedule

-   Two-way SMS: reply C to confirm; STOP and HELP handled automatically

-   Quiet hours: no SMS between 9 pm and 8 am patient local time

-   Delivery log per message: sent, delivered, failed, opened

Tracking

-   Referrer ID per source, with a tracked booking link for each

-   UTM tags (source, medium, campaign, term, content) and click IDs (gclid, fbclid) saved on the appointment

-   Thank-you page redirect for Google Ads, GA4 and Meta conversion tags

-   Tag Manager events: step viewed, reason selected, slot selected, booking completed

-   Server-side conversions (Google Ads offline conversions, Meta Conversions API) in a later phase

-   No patient health data is ever sent to ad or analytics platforms

Integrations

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Integration                                       Purpose                                     Phase
  ------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------- -------
  Email (SendGrid or Amazon SES)                    Transactional email                         2

  SMS (Twilio)                                      Texts and two-way confirm                   2

  Google Tag Manager, GA4, Google Ads, Meta Pixel   Conversion tracking                         2

  Reserve with Google                               Book from Google Search and Maps            3

  PMS (OpenDental, Dentrix, Eaglesoft)              Sync patients, appointments, availability   3

  Google Calendar, Outlook                          Provider calendar sync                      3

  Stripe                                            Deposits and paid appointments              3

  Webhooks and REST API                             Push bookings to CRM, n8n, Zapier           3
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8. Security and compliance

Production must meet HIPAA for US clinics; the prototype uses demo data only.

-   Hosting: HIPAA-eligible cloud with a signed BAA; BAAs with every vendor that touches patient data (SMS, email, storage)

-   Encryption: TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest, including file uploads

-   Clinic isolation: every record tied to one clinic and enforced at database level, so no clinic can read another's data

-   Login: email and password, 2FA, 30-minute idle timeout, strong password rules; SSO later

-   Access control: roles as in section 2; least access by default

-   Audit trail: logins, patient record views, exports, setting and template changes

-   PHI on screen and in messages: Hide PHI mode; option to leave the visit reason out of SMS

-   Consent records: SMS consent and Terms version stored with timestamp and IP

-   Abuse protection: rate limits, invisible bot check, optional OTP on the booking widget

-   Backups and uptime: daily backups kept 30 days, point-in-time restore, 99.9% uptime target

-   Patient rights: export or delete a patient's data on request; clinic-level retention period

-   Before launch: third-party penetration test and fixes

Three phases, each closed by a sign-off before the next starts.

  --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Phase                        Delivers                                                                                                                                                                                                                            Exit gate
  ---------------------------- ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ---------------------------------------------------------

  2. 10. Out of scope and open questions

Out of scope

-   Native iOS and Android apps (the widget and panels are mobile web)

-   Clinical records, charting, treatment plans, insurance claims

-   Real-time insurance eligibility checks

-   Telehealth video visits

-   Patient portal with its own login

-   Bulk marketing campaigns and review management

Open questions

-   ☐ Is "Kivi Health" the platform brand and domain?

-   ☐ Target markets: US only (HIPAA), or also India or UAE with their own data rules?

-   ☐ Does the reference flow have a Step 4 review screen, or does it book at Step 3?

-   ☐ Which PMS to integrate first?

-   ☐ Reserve with Google needs Google booking partner approval: apply directly or go through an approved partner?

-   ☐ Plans and pricing limits for Phase 2

-   ☐ SMS sender: one shared platform number pool or a number per clinic?

-   ☐ Prototype review: shareable link with demo data for clients, or internal only?
